MAIN MESSAGE
--------------------------------------------------

"AtmoGrid doesn't count posts.
It reconstructs evidence-backed weather incidents."

"We don't measure trust by how viral something is.
We measure how much genuinely independent evidence
supports the real-world event."

--------------------------------------------------
WHAT NOT TO CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------

DO NOT say:
"We are currently scraping all Indian social media."

DO NOT say:
"We are connected live to IMD radar and satellite."

DO NOT say:
"Our AI detects every fake image."

DO NOT say:
"This handles millions of events per second."

DO NOT say:
"AtmoGrid replaces IMD forecasting."

DO NOT say:
"The system issues official weather warnings."

INSTEAD say:
"This prototype demonstrates the workflow using
representative, historical, replayed and simulated
sources. The same connector and evidence architecture
is designed for authorized live providers in production."


--------------------------------------------------
IF ASKED: "IS THIS LIVE DATA?"
--------------------------------------------------

Say:
"The intelligence workflow is fully implemented as an
interactive prototype. For SIH evaluation, we use replay
and representative datasets because extreme weather
events cannot be guaranteed during a demo window, and
some platforms require restricted API access.

The same connector-and-evidence architecture is designed
to accept authorized live sources in a production
deployment."


--------------------------------------------------
IF ASKED: "HOW DO YOU DETECT FAKE REPORTS?"
--------------------------------------------------

Say:
"We avoid a single black-box fake/real label.

We evaluate provenance, geo-time consistency,
duplicate detection, media lineage, historical-media
reuse, independent-source support, and physical and
official weather context.

A suspicious evidence item is excluded without
automatically calling the entire incident false.
The recycled August 2024 image in our demo is the
clearest example of this."


--------------------------------------------------
IF ASKED: "WHAT IS UNIQUE ABOUT ATMOGRID?"
--------------------------------------------------

Say:
"Most systems collect posts. AtmoGrid determines how many
genuinely independent observations those posts represent,
then reconstructs them into evidence-backed real-world
incidents.

The key differentiators are:
- Source independence analysis (not just deduplication)
- Media lineage tracking (one video → 18 appearances → 1 contribution)
- Explainable verification (no black-box confidence score)
- Recycled media detection without invalidating the event
- Ground impact separated from weather event type
- Human authority preserved as the final verification step"


--------------------------------------------------
IF ASKED: "WHY IS THIS BIG DATA?"
--------------------------------------------------

Say:
"Weather intelligence has all four big-data properties:

Volume:   large streams of public signals
Velocity: continuous real-time incoming reports
Variety:  text, images, video, APIs, observations, warnings
Veracity: duplicates, misleading content, source independence

AtmoGrid's core contribution is improving VERACITY —
ensuring that what reaches an officer is genuinely
independent, explainable, and trustworthy — before
any operational action is taken."


--------------------------------------------------
IF ASKED: "WHY NOT JUST USE AI CONFIDENCE SCORES?"
--------------------------------------------------

Say:
"An AI confidence score can be wrong, and the officer
cannot inspect why. AtmoGrid shows the reasoning:
which independent groups agree, what physical context
supports it, what was excluded and why.

If the system says 'Highly Corroborated', the officer
can open the verification tab and check every dimension
that led to that assessment. That explainability is
essential for a system supporting official decisions."


--------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT NOTES FOR PRESENTERS
--------------------------------------------------

- The clock shows 17:33:00 IST. That is fixed.
  Say "simulated timestamp" if asked.

- Operator actions (VERIFY, DISPUTE, etc.) are local
  to this browser session. Refreshing resets them.
  This is intentional for the prototype.

- "14 simulated incidents" in the national metrics is
  the total described context. Only 5 incidents have
  full fixture data in the prototype.

- The "Demo environment" label in the sidebar and the
  "SIMULATED DATA" tag on each page are deliberate.
  They show evaluators the prototype is honest about
  its scope.

- Priorities shown (P1, P2, P3) are internal AtmoGrid
  operational categories, not official IMD warning
  severity levels.
